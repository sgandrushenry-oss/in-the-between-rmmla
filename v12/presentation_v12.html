9 · BECOMING

The student is moving.

The discipline is moving.

The institution is moving.

The material conditions are moving.

The imagined destination is moving too.

student.candidate.expert.finished.

But the person exceeds the statement.


The line may be drawn.

The sentence may carry over.

The route may be mapped.

The journey cannot be predetermined.

Becoming cannot be predetermined.
