7 · Conduction / From Big Movements to Little Movements

not replication.
not abandonment.
conduction.

How do we help students conduct overwhelming intellectual, creative, ethical, and affective energies into forms that can sustain a life of inquiry—or even just the moment of graduate school?

institutional routesoverlapping thresholdsunequal recognitioninstitutional timerecognizable state

What I have instead are little movements. Smaller practices and orientations that might make room for different forms of becoming with/in the institution.

15 · Becoming

The student is moving.

The discipline is moving.

The institution is moving.

The material conditions are moving.

The imagined destination is moving too.


There is no single threshold.

There is no finished academic self.

There may never have been a fixed other side.


The route may be mapped;

The journey cannot be predetermined.

Becoming cannot be predetermined.
