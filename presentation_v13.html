6 · The Poet / The Daemon

Become recognizable.
Make something not yet recognized.

Enter the discipline.
Alter it.

Learn its language.
Say something it has not said.

“the unrest
that is in us all”
  • the question that will not leave
  • the source that keeps bothering you
  • the connection that arrives sideways
  • the thought before you know why it belongs
  • the feeling that the argument is not “right”

Compliance is not the same thing as learning.

Stefan Zweig 243–45.

8 · Becoming

The student is moving.

The discipline is moving.

The institution is moving.

The material conditions are moving.

The imagined destination is moving too.


student.   candidate.   expert.   finished.

But the person exceeds the statement.


The line may be drawn.

The sentence may carry over.

The route may be mapped.

The journey cannot be predetermined.

Becoming cannot be predetermined.
